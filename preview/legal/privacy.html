Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

One Decision Away (“ODA”) is developed and provided by Yahya Labs. We designed ODA so that your data stays on your device as much as possible. This page explains, in plain words, what we keep, why and where.

In short

No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing.

Without an account, your saved app data stays on your device. Optional speech services and requests for online content are described below.

What stays on your device

Your name, decisions, notes, dreams, habits, evidence tree and settings are kept in the app’s storage on your device. We cannot see them.

You can download a backup or erase everything with “Delete all data” in Settings at any time.

Optional account and backup

If you sign in, our cloud database provider stores your email address and app data for backup and sync. You can sign in with a one-time email link or code, without an ODA password.

Optional third-party sign-in shares your email and, if provided, name with ODA through our cloud database provider only to create and access your account. A private relay address can replace your real email when supported. We do not request access to your contacts, email contents or files.

Your app data is used only for backup and sync. Records can be read only with your own account.

You can permanently delete your account inside the app with “Delete my account” on the Account page. This removes your account and all of your cloud data.

Reminders

In the iPhone app, reminders are scheduled on your device. On the web, if you turn on notifications, the notification address your browser provides and the times you choose are stored so we can send reminders; they are deleted when you turn notifications off.

On-device AI coach

The on-device coach runs a language model on your device. What you write to it is not sent to us or an AI company. Model files are downloaded from a public model host on first use.

On the web, optional natural speech sends the guidance text to the AI provider you configure using your own key, only after you add it in Settings and allow text sharing.

Cloud coach (account required)

Cloud AI chat and voice require an active Coach entitlement, sign-in and your permission. ODA sends your recent conversation, language choice, coach name and style, and only app context you separately enable through our server to our AI provider to generate replies. Data from your device’s health service is never sent. You can withdraw permission in Settings; data already processed cannot be recalled. Declining keeps local tools available within your subscription level. The provider’s processing terms apply.

ODA does not store your messages or the coach’s replies on its server or log them unless you explicitly share a reply for improvement or save a reflection included in optional account sync. We keep monthly counters for messages and natural cloud voice characters (the length of text submitted for speech), and a subscription verification record linked to your account. Your account ID is sent to our subscription processor to confirm your level.

Only the app data categories you switch on in “What the coach can see” are sent with your message to the AI provider to generate a reply. Categories cover your name, today’s decision and its status, your streak and days kept this week, open or missed items, course progress, dream titles, and recent meditation or sound sessions with before and after feelings when recorded. You can preview the exact capped snapshot in that sheet.

All categories are off by default. Notebook lines are included only if you turn on the separate notebook toggle, which is also off by default. You can turn off any category at any time. The opening message is generated on your device when you open the coach; it makes no request to an AI service.

AI can make mistakes. The cloud coach is not a therapist or an emergency service. Messages about self-harm, abuse or immediate danger prompt it to stop coaching and direct you to local emergency services. On-device coaching and offline actions also follow your subscription level.

Daily mood ratings stay with your app data and are included in exported backups and optional account sync. Mood sharing with the coach is off by default. If you enable Mood, the last seven days of ratings can be sent in the previewed snapshot. Comparing mood with walks also requires Evidence consent. These observed patterns do not establish a cause.

The coach is an AI for everyday life and personal growth. You can talk about relationships, family, work stress, friendships, self-doubt, breakups or conflicts, as well as your ODA decisions and progress. It offers perspectives and one small next step, not therapy or medical, medication, legal or financial advice. It is not a replacement for people in your life. If you describe abuse, violence or danger, it focuses on safety and reaching local emergency services or someone you trust.

You can name your coach and choose Beast mode, Balanced or Gentle in the coach header or Settings. The name and style are sent with cloud requests. About me is an optional profile with short fields about your life, goals, strengths, challenges, important people and values or faith. It stays out of coach requests unless you switch on its separate category in What the coach can see, which is off by default. You can preview what will be sent. Short patterns are calculated on your device only from categories you choose to share. Coach’s read on me uses only shared data and offers a tentative reflection you can correct. Corrections you save become local memory notes and are included in future coach requests only when Coach memory sharing is on. Your coach name, style, profile and explicitly saved reflections are stored on your device, included in exported backups and included in optional account sync like your other app data.

Voice is optional. On iPhone, microphone audio stays on your device for transcription. On the web, the browser speech service may process audio on its servers under its terms. ODA sends only the transcript to the coach and keeps no audio recordings. Device voice is the default; some voices may use the speech provider’s servers. If you choose natural cloud voice, the reply text goes through our server to our AI provider for speech, without microphone audio. Speak replies is off by default, and you can stop voice mode or readout at any time.

ODA starts with your supported device or browser language unless you have chosen another. The coach estimates your message language on your device; spoken replies detect the language of each reply sentence. On iPhone, Automatic compares supported installed on-device recognition languages among English, Turkish, Spanish, French, German and Italian. Availability depends on the device and installed speech models. Browser recognition uses the last detected language because its speech API accepts one language at a time. Use the voice language chip to override recognition. The reply language is sent with cloud requests.

Our AI provider’s terms apply to natural cloud voice. With an unpaid service, text and generated speech may improve the provider’s products and be reviewed by people.

Coach memory keeps up to 12 short notes about goals or patterns locally on this device, with the newest first. In What the coach can see → Coach memory, you can review, edit or delete these notes. Memory sharing is off by default. Notes are included in the limited app snapshot only when you explicitly turn on that category. Turning sharing off stops future sending; delete a note to remove the local copy.

Ratings stay on your device unless you explicitly share a reply for coach improvement. Sharing stores that one message and reply, your rating, language and coach prompt version with your account in a database hosted by our cloud database provider. No app snapshot or other chat history is attached. The exchange may contain personal details, so review it first. Shared feedback is deleted with your account; you can also request deletion.

Images

Course photos load from an external image provider, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store and processed by our subscription processor. We do not receive card details. The processor handles purchase records and an app user ID, which is your ODA account ID when signed in, to verify and restore access across devices.

The app retains an exit-offer eligibility record, including when an offer was shown and any expiry, with the member identifier. It contains no decision or notebook text. Subscription cancellation or expiry does not erase your entries. Export and account deletion remain available without paying.

Optional product analytics

Optional product analytics is off by default. If enabled in Settings, it records a random installation ID, calendar day and fixed usage events: app use, lesson completion, coach mode, audio duration range and purchase stage. Events contain no account ID, email, messages, notebook text, device health-service data or advertising ID. A dedicated endpoint operated by our cloud database provider receives them when configured; otherwise they stay on your device. This is our own product analysis, without advertising tracking. Turning it off cancels pending requests and deletes the local ID and queued events. Events already received follow the server’s configured retention period; contact support for details.

Retention and your rights

Cloud data is kept while your account exists and is deleted when you delete your account. Data on your device stays there until you delete the app or the data.

To exercise your rights to access, correct, delete, port or object under applicable data-protection law, write to support@yahyalabs.com. The data controller is Ümit Fırıldak, trading as Yahya Labs.

Children

ODA is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we update the date above and announce important changes in the app. Questions: support@yahyalabs.com

One Decision Away (“ODA”) Yahya Labs tarafından geliştirilir ve sunulur. ODA’yı, verin mümkün olduğunca senin cihazında kalacak şekilde tasarladık. Bu metin neyi, neden ve nerede sakladığımızı sade bir dille anlatır.

Kısaca

Reklam yok, izleme yok, üçüncü taraf analiz aracı yok. Verini satmıyoruz ve kimseyle pazarlama için paylaşmıyoruz.

Hesap açmadan kullandığında kayıtlı uygulama verilerin cihazında kalır. İsteğe bağlı ses hizmetleri ve çevrimiçi içerik istekleri aşağıda açıklanır.

Cihazında sakladıkların

Adın, kararların, notların, hayallerin, alışkanlıkların, kanıt ağacın ve ayarların cihazının uygulama depolamasında tutulur. Biz bunları göremeyiz.

İstediğin an Ayarlar’dan yedek indirebilir veya “Tüm verileri sil” ile hepsini silebilirsin.

İsteğe bağlı hesap ve yedekleme

Giriş yaparsan e-posta adresin ve uygulama verilerin yedekleme ve eşitleme için bulut veritabanı sağlayıcımızda saklanır. ODA şifresi olmadan, e-postana gelen tek kullanımlık bağlantı veya kodla giriş yapabilirsin.

İsteğe bağlı üçüncü taraf giriş yöntemleri, e-postanı ve sağlanmışsa adını yalnızca hesabını oluşturmak ve girişini sağlamak için bulut veritabanı sağlayıcımız üzerinden ODA ile paylaşır. Destekleniyorsa gerçek e-postan yerine özel bir yönlendirme adresi kullanabilirsin. Kişilerine, e-postalarının içeriğine veya dosyalarına erişim istemeyiz.

Uygulama verilerin yalnızca yedekleme ve eşitleme için kullanılır. Kayıtlara yalnızca kendi hesabınla erişilebilir.

Hesabını uygulamanın içinden, Hesap sayfasındaki “Hesabımı sil” ile kalıcı olarak silebilirsin. Bu, hesabını ve bulutta tutulan bütün verini siler.

Hatırlatmalar

iPhone uygulamasında hatırlatmalar cihazında planlanır. Web sürümünde bildirimleri açarsan, tarayıcının verdiği bildirim adresi ve seçtiğin saatler hatırlatma gönderebilmemiz için saklanır; bildirimleri kapatınca silinir.

Cihaz içi yapay zekâ koçu

Cihaz içi koç, cihazında çalışan bir dil modeli kullanır. Yazdıkların bize veya bir yapay zekâ şirketine gönderilmez. Model dosyaları ilk kullanımda herkese açık bir model deposundan indirilir.

Web’de isteğe bağlı doğal seslendirme, rehber metnini kendi anahtarınla yapılandırdığın yapay zekâ sağlayıcısına gönderir. Bunun için anahtarını Ayarlar’a eklemen ve metin paylaşımına izin vermen gerekir.

Bulut koçu (hesap gerekir)

Bulut yapay zekâ sohbeti ve ses için aktif Coach erişimi, giriş ve iznin gerekir. ODA son konuşmanı, dil seçimini, koç adını ve üslubunu, ayrıca yalnızca ayrı olarak açtığın uygulama bağlamını yanıt üretmek için sunucumuz üzerinden yapay zekâ sağlayıcımıza gönderir. Cihazının sağlık hizmetinden alınan veriler gönderilmez. Ayarlar’dan iznini geri alabilirsin; daha önce işlenen veriler geri çağrılamaz. Reddedersen cihaz içi araçlar abonelik seviyen kapsamında kullanılabilir. Sağlayıcının veri işleme koşulları geçerlidir.

Bir yanıtı geliştirme için açıkça paylaşmadığın veya isteğe bağlı hesap eşitlemesine dahil edilen bir değerlendirmeyi kaydetmediğin sürece ODA mesajlarını ve koç yanıtlarını sunucuda saklamaz veya kayıt tutmaz. Hesabına bağlı aylık mesaj sayacını, doğal bulut sesi karakter sayacını (seslendirme için gönderilen metnin uzunluğunu) ve abonelik doğrulama kaydını tutarız. Seviyeni doğrulamak için hesap kimliğin abonelik işlemlerini yürüten sağlayıcımıza gönderilir.

Uygulama verilerinden yalnızca “Koç neleri görebilir?” bölümünde açtığın kategoriler yanıt oluşturmak için mesajınla birlikte yapay zekâ sağlayıcısına gönderilir. Kategoriler; adın, bugünün kararı ve durumu, karar serin ve bu hafta tuttuğun günler, açık veya kaçırılan işler, kurs ilerlemen, hayal başlıkların ve varsa önceki ve sonraki hislerinle son meditasyon veya ses oturumlarındır. Política de privacidad

Última actualización: 13 de octubre de 2026

One Decision Away (“ODA”) es desarrollada y ofrecida por Yahya Labs. Diseñamos ODA para que tus datos se queden en tu dispositivo en la medida de lo posible. Aquí explicamos con claridad qué guardamos, por qué y dónde.

En resumen

Sin anuncios, sin rastreo, sin herramientas de analítica de terceros. No vendemos tus datos ni los compartimos con nadie para marketing.

Sin una cuenta, los datos guardados de la app se quedan en tu dispositivo. Los servicios de voz opcionales y las solicitudes de contenido en línea se explican más abajo.

Lo que se queda en tu dispositivo

Tu nombre, decisiones, notas, sueños, hábitos, árbol de evidencias y ajustes se guardan en el almacenamiento de la app en tu dispositivo. Nosotros no podemos verlos.

Puedes descargar una copia o borrarlo todo con “Borrar todos los datos” en Ajustes cuando quieras.

Cuenta opcional y copia de seguridad

Si inicias sesión, nuestro proveedor de base de datos en la nube guarda tu correo y los datos de la app para copias y sincronización. Puedes entrar con un enlace o código de un solo uso enviado por correo, sin contraseña de ODA.

El inicio de sesión opcional con terceros comparte tu correo y, si se facilita, tu nombre con ODA a través de nuestro proveedor de base de datos en la nube, solo para crear tu cuenta y acceder a ella. Cuando se admita, puedes usar una dirección de retransmisión privada en lugar de tu correo real. No pedimos acceso a tus contactos, contenido del correo ni archivos.

Los datos de la app solo se usan para la copia y la sincronización. Los registros solo se pueden leer con tu propia cuenta.

Puedes eliminar tu cuenta de forma permanente dentro de la app con “Eliminar mi cuenta” en la página Cuenta. Eso borra tu cuenta y todos tus datos en la nube.

Recordatorios

En la app de iPhone los recordatorios se programan en tu dispositivo. En la web, si activas las notificaciones, guardamos la dirección de notificación que da tu navegador y las horas que eliges para poder enviarlos; se borran al desactivarlas.

Coach con IA en el dispositivo

El coach en el dispositivo usa un modelo de lenguaje local. Lo que le escribes no se envía a nosotros ni a una empresa de IA. Los archivos del modelo se descargan de un repositorio público la primera vez.

En la web, la voz natural opcional envía el texto de orientación al proveedor de IA que configures con tu propia clave, solo después de añadirla en Ajustes y autorizar el intercambio de texto.

Coach en la nube (requiere cuenta)

El chat y la voz con IA en la nube requieren acceso activo a Coach, inicio de sesión y tu permiso.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

One Decision Away (“ODA”) is developed and provided by Yahya Labs. We designed ODA so that your data stays on your device as much as possible. This page explains, in plain words, what we keep, why and where.

Email your withdrawal declaration

In short

No ads, no tracking across apps. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing.

Without an account, your saved app data stays on your device. Optional speech services and requests for online content are described below.

What stays on your device

Your name, decisions, notes, dreams, habits, evidence tree and settings stay on this device by default. They leave it only if you enable cloud backup or cloud coach, with separate permission for each. We never sell them.

You can download a backup or erase everything with “Reset all data” in Settings at any time.

Optional account and backup

If you sign in, your email address and app data are stored in our database, hosted by our hosting and database provider, to back them up and keep your devices in sync. You do not need an ODA password: you can sign in with a one-time link or code sent to your email.

When available, you can also choose Apple or Google sign-in. These methods share the email address and, if provided, name needed for your account with ODA through our hosting and database provider. We use these details only to create your account and sign you in. With Apple, you can share a private relay address instead of your actual email address. We do not request access to your contacts, email contents or files.

Backup records can be read only with your own account. The categories you separately choose to share with the coach are described below.

Optional account backup can include your photos, mood and energy check-ins, movement sessions, savings and cost or income plans, and any values or faith you enter in your coach profile. These records are linked to your account. Cloud coach sharing has separate category controls.

You can permanently delete your account inside the app with “Delete my account” on the Account page, without paying. This removes your ODA account and its cloud app records.

Apple, Google Play and our subscription management provider process purchases and may retain billing records required by law. Deleting your ODA account does not cancel a store subscription. Manage it separately in the store where you subscribed.

Reminders

In the iPhone and Android apps, reminders are scheduled on your device. Android delivery times are approximate and may vary with battery settings. On the web, if you enable push reminders, we store your browser’s notification address and chosen times to send them. Turning notifications off deletes these details.

On-device AI coach

Offline guidance in the iOS app runs on your device and does not download a language model. The web version offers an optional on-device language model; its files are downloaded from our model-file hosting provider only when you enable it. What you write to local guidance is not sent to us or to an AI company.

On the web, if you add your own Google Gemini key in Settings and allow text sharing, the guidance text to be read aloud is sent to Google with your key for natural voices. This is optional.

Cloud coach (account required)

Cloud AI chat and natural cloud voice are for adults aged 18 or over with an eligible, unprotected profile, sign-in, the required Coach access and separate cloud AI consent. With your permission, requests go through our server to a third-party AI provider. The server checks age band and protected-profile status. Only context categories you separately enable are added; all are off by default. HealthKit data is never sent to the coach.

Cloud AI chat and voice require an active Coach entitlement and sign-in. The conversation you send (recent messages and language choice) goes to our server and a third-party AI provider to generate a reply. The provider’s processing terms apply.

ODA does not store your messages or the coach’s replies on its server or log them unless you explicitly share a reply for improvement or save a reflection that is included in optional account sync. Otherwise, we keep monthly usage counters for messages (how many you have used) and natural cloud voice characters (the length of text submitted for speech) and a subscription verification snapshot, tied to your account. To confirm your level, your account ID is sent to our subscription management provider.

Only the app data categories you switch on in “What the coach can see” are sent with your message to the AI provider to generate a reply. Categories cover your name, today’s decision and its status, your streak and days kept this week, open or missed items, course progress, dream titles, and recent meditation or sound sessions with before and after feelings when recorded. You can preview the exact capped snapshot in that sheet.

All categories are off by default. Notebook lines are included only if you turn on the separate notebook toggle, which is also off by default. You can turn off any category at any time. The opening message is generated on your device when you open the coach; it makes no request to an AI service.

The cloud coach is an AI, not a therapist or an emergency service. If a message mentions self-harm, abuse or immediate danger, the coach stops coaching and encourages you to contact local emergency services (911 in the US, 112 in Türkiye). Access to on-device coaching and offline actions also follows your subscription level.

Daily mood ratings stay with your app data and are included in exported backups and optional account sync. Mood sharing with the coach is off by default. If you enable Mood, the last seven days of ratings can be sent in the previewed snapshot. Comparing mood with walks also requires Evidence consent. These observed patterns do not establish a cause.

With your permission, ODA sends your recent conversation, selected language, coach name and style, and only the app context you separately enable to our server hosted by our hosting and database provider and then a third-party AI provider to generate replies. AI can make mistakes. It does not provide medical care or emergency help. HealthKit data is never sent. Declining keeps the local tools available. You can withdraw permission here at any time; data already processed cannot be recalled. To check age eligibility, ODA saves your declared age range and protected-profile status privately with your ODA account with our hosting and database provider. Your declaration is not independently verified.

For cloud AI eligibility, this account-linked age declaration is stored with our hosting and database provider even when optional account backup and Coach Memory sharing are off.

The coach is an AI for everyday life and personal growth. You can talk about relationships, family, work stress, friendships, self-doubt, breakups or conflicts, as well as your ODA decisions and progress. It offers perspectives and one small next step, not therapy or medical, medication, legal or financial advice. It is not a replacement for people in your life. If you describe abuse, violence or danger, it focuses on safety and reaching local emergency services or someone you trust.

You can name your coach and choose Beast mode, Balanced or Gentle in the coach header or Settings. The name and style are sent with cloud requests. About me is an optional profile with short fields about your life, goals, strengths, challenges, important people and values or faith. It stays out of coach requests unless you switch on its separate category in What the coach can see, which is off by default. You can preview what will be sent. Short patterns are calculated on your device only from categories you choose to share. Coach’s read on me uses only shared data and offers a tentative reflection you can correct. Corrections you save become local memory notes and are included in future coach requests only when Coach memory sharing is on. Your coach name, style, profile and explicitly saved reflections are stored on your device, included in exported backups and included in optional account sync like your other app data.

Voice is optional. On iPhone, transcription requires on-device speech recognition, so microphone audio stays on your device. On the web, your browser speech service may process audio on its servers under its terms. ODA does not send your audio to the coach or store an audio recording. Only the transcript is sent to the coach, like a typed message. Device voice is the default and uses your device or browser speech service; some voices may use the provider’s servers. If you choose Natural cloud voice in Settings, the coach reply text is sent through our server to a third-party AI provider to create speech. Your microphone audio is not sent to the AI provider for this readout. Speak replies is off by default. Use End to leave voice mode, or turn off Speak replies to stop readout in text chat.

ODA starts with your supported device or browser language unless you have chosen another. The coach estimates your message language on your device; spoken replies detect the language of each reply sentence. On iPhone, Automatic compares supported installed on-device recognition languages among English, Turkish, Spanish, French, German and Italian. Availability depends on the device and installed speech models. Browser recognition uses the last detected language because its speech API accepts one language at a time. Use the voice language chip to override recognition. The reply language is sent with cloud requests.

The third-party AI provider’s terms apply. With an unpaid service, text and generated speech may be used to improve the provider’s products and reviewed by people.

Coach memory keeps up to 12 short notes about goals or patterns locally on this device, with the newest first. In What the coach can see → Coach memory, you can review, edit or delete these notes. Memory sharing is off by default. Notes are included in the limited app snapshot only when you explicitly turn on that category. Turning sharing off stops future sending; delete a note to remove the local copy. Setup answers stay in a separate device store and outside backups. When this category is on, the coach can also read your life, mental state, coping, duration and habit choices. Use Privacy in Settings to delete those answers.

Thumbs up and down ratings stay locally on your device unless you choose to share. Only if you explicitly choose Share this reply to improve the coach do we store that single member message and coach reply, your rating, the language and the coach prompt version, linked to your account in our database hosted by our hosting and database provider, for coach improvement. No app snapshot or other conversation history is attached. The exchange itself may contain personal details you wrote or the coach mentioned, so review it before sharing. Shared feedback is removed when you delete your account; you can also contact us about deletion.

If you send a report, ODA stores part of the reply, your language and its feature category with our hosting and database provider, linked to your account, to review safety and improve filters. Your message and app snapshot are not included. Reports are deleted with your ODA account. Contact support to request their deletion separately.

Images

Course photos load from our image provider. As with any web request, our image provider sees your IP address in order to deliver the photo.

App Store and Google Play

Apple processes App Store payments; Google processes Google Play payments. Our subscription management provider processes purchase records and the app user identifier to verify subscriptions and restore access. For signed-in members this is the ODA account ID. For web purchases, our subscription management and payment providers process account, email, payment and subscription records. Payment providers handle card details; ODA does not receive full card details. Necessary billing records are separate from optional product analytics and may be retained for billing or legal obligations.

The app retains an exit-offer eligibility record, including when an offer was shown and any expiry, with the member identifier. It contains no decision or notebook text. Subscription cancellation or expiry does not erase your entries. Export and account deletion remain available without paying.

Optional product analytics

If you enable product analytics in Settings, ODA records a random installation identifier, calendar day, and fixed app interaction events such as app use, lesson completion, coach mode, sound session duration bucket and purchase funnel stage. They contain no account ID, email, message, journal text, HealthKit data or advertising identifier. A dedicated endpoint operated by our hosting and database provider receives these events; without it they remain on your device. This is first-party product analysis, not advertising tracking. Analytics is off until you enable it. Turning it off cancels pending requests and removes the local identifier and queued events. Already received events are retained according to our configured server retention, which is described by support on request.

Account and billing data support the contract; security and abuse prevention support legitimate interests; legally required records support legal obligations. Optional analytics and cloud AI require your separate consent. You can withdraw consent for future processing in Settings without paying.

You can export the app backup from Settings without paying. The backup contains the supported saved app records, not every draft, downloaded file or billing record. For access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection under GDPR or KVKK, email support@yahyalabs.com. You can complain to your competent data protection authority, including the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority where applicable.

Active account records are removed when the server confirms account deletion. Infrastructure backups may retain copies until their configured retention expires; billing providers may retain legally required transaction records. Device copies and exported backups remain until you erase them. Analytics events are not linked to your ODA account and follow their separate configured retention. Ask support for the applicable retention periods.

Our hosting and database provider, our subscription management provider, Apple, Google and other providers described here may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Contact support for processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards. We do not sell personal data, use advertising identifiers or track you across apps. Optional product analytics stays off until you enable it.

Immediate service and online withdrawal

Your name, contract details, email and withdrawal declaration go to ODA’s server hosted by our hosting and database provider to record and handle your declaration and prepare an acknowledgement email. To protect the service and prevent abuse, ODA’s backend hosted by our hosting and database provider also uses a secret salt to turn your IP address and email into repeatable hash keys. These pseudonymous keys are stored with an hourly rate-limit window’s start time and a request count to limit submissions. The receipt is also kept on this device until you clear it. Do not include card details, passwords or sign-in codes.

Deleting your ODA account does not automatically delete your withdrawal declaration, the prepared acknowledgement email or the rate-limit security records stored on ODA’s server. No automatic deletion date is set for the declaration or prepared email. Security rows are cleaned only when a valid submission reaches the database, including retries. Rows whose hourly window started more than two hours before the current hour are removed; without further submissions, they can remain. Owner decision pending: retention period or criteria and erasure for withdrawal records and idle security rows. Contact support about your erasure rights. “Clear this device’s receipt” removes only the copy on this device.

Workout history and age bands

Your saved workout history includes exercises, completed sets, repetitions or timed holds, loads, session times and duration, totals and any saved program reference. It is included in exported backups and sent to our hosting and database provider when you use account backup or sync. Unsaved workout drafts and separately stored program enrollments are not included in that backup. A program reference saved with a workout is included.

Your declared age band and whether your profile is protected are saved with your profile and included in exported backups and optional account sync. Your saved work schedule is also included. Your survey draft and the separate plan cache stay on your device and are not included in that backup. We do not collect a date of birth.

If you are an adult eligible to use the cloud coach and have completed the survey, you can turn on Coach memory in What the coach can see. This allows your age band and selected plan summaries, including goals, habits, obstacles and sleep choices, to be sent with a cloud coach request to a third-party AI provider. Separate cloud AI permission is also required. Memory sharing is off by default and is independent of account backup. Your survey answers about gender and how you found ODA are not added to the automatic plan summary.

Cookies and browser storage

ODA uses browser storage to remember your language, appearance, sign-in session and saved app records. These support the features you request. Hosted sign-in and payment pages may also use storage needed to secure their own services. ODA does not use advertising cookies or tracking across apps.

Optional usage statistics stay off until you choose to share. The website and app use the same analytics preference for this browser. Accepting statistics does not permit cloud backup, cloud AI or marketing. You can decline and keep using the site, or change your choice through Privacy choices or Settings > Privacy.

Your preference stays in this browser until you change it or clear its storage. Turning statistics off cancels pending requests and removes the local analytics identifier and queued events. Events already received by the analytics service follow its configured retention; contact support for details. Other browsers and devices have their own preference.

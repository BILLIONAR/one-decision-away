What the research says.

A practice window you can commit to. Sources you can open and question.

Why 66 days?

Lally, van Jaarsveld, Potts & Wardle (2010) · European Journal of Social Psychology

Lally et al. (2010) reported a median modelled time of 66 days for habits to approach automaticity, with estimates from 18 to 254 days in well-fitting models. The study used self-reports; the longest estimate extended beyond observation.

Read the study

Why record your decisions?

Harkin et al. (2016) · Psychological Bulletin

Harkin et al. (2016) found that monitoring goal progress supported goal attainment on average across experimental studies. ODA gives you a place to record your practice.

Read the study

Neither study tested ODA. Your 66-day plan is time for practice, not a promise of results by day 66. Results vary.

Before you begin.

Will I change my life in 66 days?

66 days gives your practice a clear window. It does not guarantee a habit or a particular result. Start small, use your check-ins and adjust as you go. Your daily practice can continue while subscribed after day 66.

What am I paying for?

Using ODA after setup requires a paid Essentials, Pro or Coach subscription. The App Store or Google Play shows the full price, billing period and renewal terms before you buy. Manage or cancel in the store where you subscribed. Cancelling keeps access until the paid period ends; deleting your ODA account does not cancel your subscription.

What happens to my data?

Your survey answers are saved on your device. Cloud backup is optional. Usage statistics stay off until you enable them; if enabled, ODA can receive your optional answer about how you found the app. Cloud Coach receives only the context you separately allow. Read the privacy policy for details.

What should I expect from the AI coach?

Coach membership adds AI coaching. AI can be wrong. Cloud use needs an account, separate consent and an available service, with usage limits. ODA is a personal development tool, not a therapist or emergency service.

Can I access my data without renewing?

Yes. Restore purchases, data export, account deletion, support and legal pages remain reachable without a subscription. After expiry, your entries are retained for export; app features require an active subscription. Use Account → Delete my account, or the account deletion link below.

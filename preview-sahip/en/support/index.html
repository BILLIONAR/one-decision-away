ODA · ONE DECISION AWAY

A little help, when you need it.

ODA is built around one small decision you can keep. Find a practical answer below, or reach us directly.

Contact ODA

For app issues, feedback, accessibility requests or questions about your account, email us.

Write an email

support@yahyalabs.com

Please describe what happened, what you expected, your device and app or browser version. A screenshot can help if you remove private information first.

Never send passwords, sign-in codes, API keys, payment details or your full backup. Your notebook and reflections can stay private.

Practical answers

Protect or move your progress

Before resetting the app or changing devices, export a JSON backup from Settings → Backup & sync. Keep the file somewhere you trust.

On the receiving device, use Restore under Backup & sync and choose your backup file. Restoring replaces the data on that device, so export its existing data first if you need it. An optional account offers cloud sync when it is configured; a local backup remains useful.

Continue a course

Open Courses and choose the course you were working on. Return to your next lesson, try the exercise in your real day, and record what you noticed.

A missed day does not erase learning. If a lesson is locked in the iPhone app, check the available subscription level or use Restore purchases in Upgrade.

Make reminders work for you

Turn reminders on in Settings and allow notifications when your device asks. On iPhone, also check Settings → Notifications → ODA; Focus or Scheduled Summary may delay delivery.

iPhone reminders are scheduled locally for the week ahead and refreshed when you return to ODA. On the web, open-tab reminders need the app running; background push requires a supported browser, permission and configured cloud service.

Restore or manage an iPhone subscription

Open Upgrade → Restore purchases using the Apple account that made the purchase. If access does not return, email us with the product name and what you see. Do not send payment details.

Manage or cancel renewal in your iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. ODA’s website does not sell subscriptions.

Sign in or delete an account

On iPhone, sign in with an email code or an existing account password. On the web, Apple or Google sign-in may also be offered. Use the same email address or sign-in method when returning.

Request a fresh sign-in email and check spam or junk. In the iPhone app, enter the six-digit code from the latest email. Never share that code.

Open Account → Delete my account to delete your ODA account and cloud app records without a subscription. This does not cancel an Apple or Google Play subscription. Manage it separately in the store where you subscribed. To clear this device too, use Settings → Reset all data. Export a backup first if you want a copy.

Choose what your ODA coach can see

The coach is an AI for everyday life and personal growth. You can talk about relationships, family, work stress, friendships, self-doubt, breakups or conflicts, as well as your ODA decisions and progress. It offers perspectives and one small next step, not therapy or medical, medication, legal or financial advice. It is not a replacement for people in your life. If you describe abuse, violence or danger, it focuses on safety and reaching local emergency services or someone you trust.

Open “What the coach can see” to choose categories and preview exactly which app data will accompany your message. All categories are off by default; notebook lines need their own separate toggle.

When you send a cloud message, your typed conversation and only the app categories you switched on go to the configured AI provider (Google Gemini or OpenAI) to generate a reply. The opening nudge is made on your device without an AI request. If the cloud is unavailable, a local nudge still helps you choose a small next step.

You can name your coach and choose Beast mode, Balanced or Gentle in the coach header or Settings. The name and style are sent with cloud requests. About me is an optional profile with short fields about your life, goals, strengths, challenges, important people and values or faith. It stays out of coach requests unless you switch on its separate category in What the coach can see, which is off by default. You can preview what will be sent. Short patterns are calculated on your device only from categories you choose to share. Coach’s read on me uses only shared data and offers a tentative reflection you can correct. Corrections you save become local memory notes and are included in future coach requests only when Coach memory sharing is on. Your coach name, style, profile and explicitly saved reflections are stored on your device, included in exported backups and included in optional account sync like your other app data.

Voice is optional. On iPhone, transcription requires on-device speech recognition, so microphone audio stays on your device. On the web, your browser speech service may process audio on its servers under its terms. ODA does not send your audio to the coach or store an audio recording. Only the transcript is sent to the coach, like a typed message. Device voice is the default and uses your device or browser speech service; some voices may use the provider’s servers. If you choose Natural cloud voice in Settings, the coach reply text is sent through our server to Google Gemini to create speech. Your microphone audio is not sent to Google for this readout. Speak replies is off by default. Use End to leave voice mode, or turn off Speak replies to stop readout in text chat.

Coach memory keeps up to 12 short notes about goals or patterns locally on this device, with the newest first. In What the coach can see → Coach memory, you can review, edit or delete these notes. Memory sharing is off by default. Notes are included in the limited app snapshot only when you explicitly turn on that category. Turning sharing off stops future sending; delete a note to remove the local copy. Setup answers stay in a separate device store and outside backups. When this category is on, the coach can also read your life, mental state, coping, duration and habit choices. Use Privacy in Settings to delete those answers.

Thumbs up and down ratings stay locally on your device unless you choose to share. Only if you explicitly choose Share this reply to improve the coach do we store that single member message and coach reply, your rating, the language and the coach prompt version, linked to your account in our Supabase database, for coach improvement. No app snapshot or other conversation history is attached. The exchange itself may contain personal details you wrote or the coach mentioned, so review it before sharing. Shared feedback is removed when you delete your account; you can also contact us about deletion.

Choose a more natural coach voice or change its language

Open Settings → Coach voice. Device voice is the default. Choose Natural cloud voice to opt in, then choose a warm, calm or firm voice and use Preview voice to hear a short sample. Natural cloud voice requires a signed-in account and an available cloud service. Downloaded device voices only appear if your browser or device makes them available to ODA.

Natural voice sends the coach reply text to Google to create speech. Your audio is not sent to Google for this.

Device voice uses your device or browser speech service. Some voices use the provider’s servers under its terms.

Google’s terms apply. With an unpaid service, text and generated speech may improve Google products and be reviewed by people.

If you’re offline, reach the monthly limit or natural voice is unavailable, your coach uses the best available device voice.

ODA starts with your supported device or browser language unless you have chosen another. The coach estimates your message language on your device; spoken replies detect the language of each reply sentence. On iPhone, Automatic compares supported installed on-device recognition languages among English, Turkish, Spanish, French, German and Italian. Availability depends on the device and installed speech models. Browser recognition uses the last detected language because its speech API accepts one language at a time. Use the voice language chip to override recognition. The reply language is sent with cloud requests.

On iPhone, download an Enhanced or Premium voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices (Read & Speak on newer iOS). Choose your language. Available voices depend on your device and browser.

Know what ODA can help with

ODA offers educational exercises, reflection and tools for everyday personal growth. It does not diagnose, provide medical or psychological treatment, or guarantee a particular result.

If you need urgent help or feel in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or someone you trust. This support inbox is not an emergency service.

# Official store badges

Downloaded on 2026-10-10, without modifying artwork. Localized for en, tr, es, fr, de and it. These are free store marketing assets, subject to each store's identity guidelines and trademark terms, not newly generated images.

- Apple: `https://tools.applemediaservices.com/api/badges/download-on-the-app-store/black/{en-us,tr-tr,es-es,fr-fr,de-de,it-it}?size=250x83`
- Apple usage: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/
- Google: `https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/images/generic/{en,tr,es,fr,de,it}_badge_web_generic.png`
- Google resources: https://google.play/business/brand-and-marketing/

Apple appears first, with the original black badge and at least 12px clear space around its 48px height. Google PNGs retain their transparent clear space and display at 72px overall height, matching Apple's visible badge height. Localized Apple and Google trademark credits appear once in the legal footer. The owner must approve actual listing URLs in `src/data/landingStoreLinks.ts`; no store destination is active by default.

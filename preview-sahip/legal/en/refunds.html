Refunds and cancellation

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Yahya Labs explains how to stop renewal and request help with a payment for ODA.

Email your withdrawal declaration

App Store

App Store subscriptions are billed by Apple to your Apple Account (Apple ID) when you confirm the purchase. They renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple may charge for renewal within the final 24 hours. The purchase screen shows the subscription period, full price and any introductory terms before you agree.

Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings > your name / Apple ID > Subscriptions > ODA. Apple handles App Store billing and refund decisions under its refund policy. Request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem service. Cancellation normally leaves access until the paid period ends and does not itself refund earlier payments.

Google Play

Android purchases use Google Play billing. Payment is charged to your Google account when confirmed. The subscription renews automatically at the disclosed price and billing interval until cancelled. Cancel before the next renewal in Play Store > profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > ODA. You normally keep access until the paid period ends.

Google Play purchases follow Google Play’s refund policy and applicable consumer law. Use Google Play’s refund request route; eligibility depends on the purchase and your country. Cancellation does not automatically refund a payment. Contact support if the store directs you to the developer.

Web subscriptions

When web checkout is available, it shows the plan, currency, full price, billing period and renewal terms before confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled.

For a web purchase, open subscription management from your account to reach the billing portal, or use the management link in your purchase email. Cancel before the next renewal. Access normally continues until the paid period ends; an approved refund may end it earlier. If the portal is unavailable or you have lost access, email support@yahyalabs.com. Deleting an account or uninstalling ODA does not cancel any subscription.

Write to support@yahyalabs.com from your purchase email address with your receipt or transaction reference and the payment you want reviewed. Do not send card details, passwords or sign-in codes. We review the request under the applicable consumer law and explain the outcome. Cancellation alone does not refund a payment; an approved refund may end paid access.

Withdrawal and consumer rights

For an eligible web distance contract, consumers in the EU and Türkiye have 14 days from concluding the contract to withdraw without giving a reason. Send an unambiguous withdrawal request to support@yahyalabs.com before the period ends, identifying the account and purchase. Where withdrawal applies, the refund is made within 14 days of notice using the original payment method unless you agree otherwise, subject to mandatory law.

For digital content supplied immediately without a physical medium, loss of the withdrawal right applies only where the law permits it, supply has begun, and you have separately given prior express consent to immediate supply and acknowledged losing that right. Any required contract confirmation must also be provided. Accepting these terms alone is not that consent.

An ongoing digital service does not lose its EU withdrawal right merely because access begins. If you expressly request service during the withdrawal period, a lawful proportionate charge for service already supplied may apply; loss of the right for a fully performed service requires the legally required consent and acknowledgement. Mandatory Turkish rights and any more favourable local rules remain protected.

You may use this wording, but no special form is required: “I withdraw from my ODA purchase. Account email: [...]. Purchase / receipt reference: [...]. Purchase date: [...]. Name: [...]. Date: [...].” Do not send card details or sign-in codes. These terms do not limit remedies for faulty or misdescribed digital content or services, or access to your local consumer authority or court.

Immediate service and online withdrawal

ODA subscriptions provide an ongoing service. At checkout, you expressly request that performance begin during the 14-day withdrawal period. Starting access does not waive your withdrawal right for the subscription. If you withdraw, a lawful proportionate payment for service supplied before withdrawal may apply. No subscription-wide digital-content waiver is collected.

Use “Withdraw from contract here” to enter your name, contract or purchase details and email address for the declaration, review the declaration, then choose “Confirm withdrawal”. The function is available without payment, sign-in or account deletion, including when new purchases are unavailable. You can also send an unambiguous declaration to support@yahyalabs.com before your applicable deadline. The acknowledgement records your declaration and the server submission time; keep the downloadable copy. Any refund and resulting billing changes are handled under applicable law.

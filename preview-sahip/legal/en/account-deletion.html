Delete your ODA account

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Yahya Labs. This public page explains how to delete your ODA account on iOS and Android and how to request help if you have lost access. Reading it and emailing a request do not require login or a subscription.

Email your withdrawal declaration
Contact support: support@yahyalabs.com

iPhone and Android

Open ODA > Me > Account > Delete my account. Sign in to the account you want to delete, then confirm. Nothing is deleted before confirmation. You do not need to pay or cancel a subscription first.

Can’t sign in?

If you have lost access, contact support to request deletion without signing in or installing the app. We verify account ownership before deleting. Never send a password or sign-in code.

What is deleted

Export a backup from Settings before confirming if you want a copy. Account deletion covers account-linked backups, profile and coach memory saved in sync, shared coach feedback and reports, and associated cloud app records. It does not erase exported files, other devices or purchase records held by billing providers.

When deletion takes effect

The in-app request removes the sign-in account and associated active cloud records during the confirmed server operation. After success is confirmed, ODA clears personal app data from the device that made the request, unless ODA could not load data on this device. In that case, your account and cloud backup are deleted, but the unreadable device copy is left unchanged for you to recover or remove. Before removing it, try downloading a recovery copy. To remove the local copy, clear ODA’s site data in your browser settings; on Android, clear ODA’s app data in system settings; on iPhone or iPad, use Delete App for ODA. If the server cannot confirm deletion or you are offline, account deletion is not complete: retry or contact support. For an email request, support first verifies account ownership and then confirms the result; no fixed completion time is promised here.

What stays and what to cancel

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple or Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in the store where you subscribed. Our subscription management and payment providers may retain purchase records for billing or legal obligations. Copies in infrastructure backups may remain until the normal retention period ends. Exported backups and local copies on other devices remain until you erase them. Deleting your ODA account does not automatically delete your withdrawal declaration, the prepared acknowledgement email or the rate-limit security records stored on ODA’s server. No automatic deletion date is set for the declaration or prepared email. Security rows are cleaned only when a valid submission reaches the database, including retries. Rows whose hourly window started more than two hours before the current hour are removed; without further submissions, they can remain. No retention period or erasure procedure has been defined yet for withdrawal records or security records left when there are no further submissions. Contact support about your erasure rights. “Clear this device’s receipt” removes only the copy on this device.

Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings > your name / Apple ID > Subscriptions > ODA. Apple handles App Store billing and refund decisions under its refund policy. Request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem service. Cancellation normally leaves access until the paid period ends and does not itself refund earlier payments.

Android purchases use Google Play billing. Payment is charged to your Google account when confirmed. The subscription renews automatically at the disclosed price and billing interval until cancelled. Cancel before the next renewal in Play Store > profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > ODA. You normally keep access until the paid period ends.

For a web purchase, open subscription management from your account to reach the billing portal, or use the management link in your purchase email. Cancel before the next renewal. Access normally continues until the paid period ends; an approved refund may end it earlier. If the portal is unavailable or you have lost access, email support@yahyalabs.com. Deleting an account or uninstalling ODA does not cancel any subscription.

Account deletion does not cancel a web subscription. Cancel separately through the management link in your purchase email. Turn them off in Privacy choices to remove the local analytics identifier and any events waiting to be sent.

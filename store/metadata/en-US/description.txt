ODA turns big goals into one small decision you can keep today.

Each morning, write today’s one decision. Keep it small enough to start in two minutes. Mark it when it’s done. Every promise you keep grows a leaf on your evidence tree: motivation usually follows action, and ODA shows you that every day.

WHAT’S INSIDE
• Today’s one decision, with a 30-second plan for obstacles
• The evidence tree and a weekly look-back
• A notebook, your dreams and three small habits
• 18 guided courses: procrastination, focus, sleep, calm, confidence, self-compassion, meditation, self-suggestion and more
• The Sound Room: sounds for calm, sleep, focus and breath
• An AI coach that runs on your device, so what you write stays on your phone (it downloads a language model of about 1.1 GB once, only when you choose to start it)
• Gentle reminders: at most two a day
• English, Turkish and Spanish

FREE, AND STAYING FREE
Today’s decision, the evidence tree, the notebook, the coach and your backup are always free. So are the one-day starter course, the first two lessons of every course and the first two sounds in every Sound Room category.

# ODA: a personal practice, with evidence you can return to

The audience is someone who wants to follow through, feels overwhelmed by ambitious growth systems, and needs one usable next step. The product should help them choose an action, make a small start, learn from the attempt, and return without shame. Course completion and actual practice are separate records.

## Decisions implemented

- Preserve the owner's original C4 mark and designer signature. Use forest `#173E35`, wine `#8A3042`, and ivory `#F7F3EA` consistently. Keep forest navigation and readable paper surfaces; reserve depth for the landing's original doorway illustration.
- First use explains the daily loop, allows optional intentions and dreams, preserves interrupted setup locally, and distinguishes fictional Dream Dollars from money.
- Preserve the substantial 18-course curriculum. Add course-specific experiment artifacts, cue/action/fallback plans, attempts, reflection, and reviews across English, Turkish, and Spanish. These are private user records, included in backups, rather than analytics.
- Returning users see their actual decision, course continuation, and evidence. Weekly reflection produces an actionable change rather than a performance score.
- Keep everyday reading fast. Lightweight generated course metadata serves previews and restore validation; full course text belongs to the course route.
- Preserve existing pricing/entitlement behavior. The value of deeper paid levels is access to applicable course tools and content, not a guarantee of personal transformation. No payment configuration or external AI service was purchased or enabled.

## Public references reviewed on 30 September 2026

- [GOV.UK task lists](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/task-list/): distinguish state from navigation; save and resume ordered multi-session work. ODA's course and onboarding applications are our design inferences.
- [Self-Determination Theory](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/the-theory/): voluntary choice and competence informed optional planning and a non-punitive review. This does not establish ODA's effectiveness.
- [Opal brand kit](https://brandkit.opal.so/): a reference for restraint around dimensional illustration. No brand assets or layouts were copied.
- [Apple tab bars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars): the independent design research supplied stable labeled destinations as a reference. The root browsing tool returned the page’s JavaScript requirement; no direct quotation or full-page review is claimed.
- [Web animation guidance](https://web.dev/articles/animations-guide): use transform/opacity sparingly and respect reduced motion. No continuous parallax or heavyweight 3D runtime was introduced.

Pinterest direct search and pin/board access were blocked. Independent domain-filtered research retrieved public editorial board metadata; see docs/LANDING_DESIGN_2026-09-30.md for exact links and access limits. Individual pins are not claimed as reviewed sources. Course evidence remains in `docs/COURSE_EVIDENCE.md`; new planning/review components cite their evidence and limitations in the course UI.

## Buyer and user acceptance checks

Can a new visitor explain what to do today? Can they leave and resume without losing work? Does a returning learner recover the next lesson and their personal experiment? Does a backup retain course reflections, practice and daily data? Can keyboard and small-screen users finish the same work? The accompanying browser QA and regression suite exercise these outcomes; they do not certify clinical effects, App Store acceptance, or device behavior.

ONE ART DIRECTION
A real route into possibility. The full entrance is one cinematic photograph. Golden-lit rock and the path at right create forward momentum while the left carries a strong, calm headline. Use the approved gold/ruby domino logo as-is. ODA means ONE DECISION AWAY; do not introduce rooms or doors as a wordplay concept. Do not imply the photograph shows an ODA user or a product result.

PALETTE
Deep burgundy #54010C: navigation surface, photo scrim, dark text on gold controls
Ruby #C51624: supporting active state, not an additional competing hero accent
Gold #E0AB5A: primary action, small accents, focus ring, selected inline headline emphasis
Warm ivory #F7F3EA: hero text, neutral lower sections
Remove the current lavender arch, purple CTA and purple highlight from the entrance. Do not broadly recolor the photograph into monochrome; the natural gold light should remain visible.

COMPOSITION
- Desktop: full-width photograph from the navigation zone through the first fold; roughly 760–860px entrance height at a 1440px viewport, content-driven rather than a fixed clipping box
- Keep content in a max-width 1240px grid; copy left, max-width about 600px; reserve the right half for the lit peaks/path
- Begin with object-fit cover and object-position 65% 50%; the supplied image is almost 16:9, so a broad hero preserves most of it
- Use a localized deep-burgundy horizontal scrim: strong beneath left copy, fade over the central third, very light at right. Keep the lower-right road visible, with a subtle bottom fade for a clean section transition
- Navigation: dark burgundy or burgundy-translucent surface, ivory links, intact approved logo, restrained gold border/CTA; verify menu contrast independently
- Keep live heading and buttons as HTML. Do not bake text or fake interface into the photograph
- Move the existing interactive example card to the next ivory section so the hero remains genuinely photo-led. Preserve its working three-step interaction and clearly identified example status
- Keep primary CTA wording/function and account-free/local-storage caveats accurate. Preserve language switching and translations

MOBILE
- Start with right-side focal positioning around 94% 50% so the illuminated main peak and the curving path survive the narrow crop
- Use an adaptive top-to-bottom burgundy scrim, strongest behind the headline and controls, rather than a uniform dark veil
- Let the hero expand with text and language; do not force 100vh and cut off buttons or caveats
- Keep real landscape visibly exposed beyond the copy. If small screens hide the path behind text, increase the image-led lower portion or adapt the background placement; do not shrink typography just to retain a desktop composition
- Header and hero controls must fit at 360px without horizontal scrolling; tap targets at least 44px

ACCESSIBILITY / PERFORMANCE ACCEPTANCE
- Solid ivory on burgundy is 13.68:1; gold on burgundy is 7.31:1; ivory on ruby is 5.40:1. These are solid-color calculations, not proof of photo-overlay contrast
- Measure final rendered text/photograph contrast at the actual crop: 4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text and essential UI boundaries
- Visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, no parallax requirement, and logical reading order
- Original is 3243×1842 JPEG, 639,103 bytes. Self-host a responsive optimized derivative in the existing application; preserve this original and credit record. Do not hotlink an external photo CDN for production
- Hero is an LCP candidate: preload only the actual chosen responsive source, do not lazy-load it, declare dimensions/aspect ratio and avoid duplicate downloads
- Treat a purely decorative background as empty alt; if implemented as meaningful image content, use concise factual alt describing the mountain trail and golden sunrise
- Check 360/390/768/1440 widths plus longer translations, image loading failure, keyboard navigation, and existing CTA/demo flows before presenting the result

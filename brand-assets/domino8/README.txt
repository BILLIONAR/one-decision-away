ODA ICON 8 — REFERENCE-GUIDED RECONSTRUCTION
Created: 3 October 2026

PROVENANCE
This package contains a newly generated, reference-guided reconstruction of the
selected ODA domino icon 8. It is not the recovered original image and does not
contain the original image's bytes. The available reference was the large icon
within ODA-8-Telefon-Onizleme.png (980 × 820 pixels). The original standalone
high-resolution source was unavailable.

PRODUCTION SOURCE
ODA-Icon8-Reconstruction-Master-1254.png
Actual size: 1254 × 1254 pixels; 8-bit RGB PNG; sRGB; fully opaque.
Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The image is not an enlarged
copy of the small reference crop. This is the highest-resolution output returned
by that generation. A larger size was requested, but 1254 × 1254 was returned.
The source is retained byte-for-byte as returned; no resampling or image edits
were applied to this master after generation.

DESIGN AND PLATFORM USE
Three unmarked polished domino shapes: one gold foreground piece leaning left
and two upright ruby/burgundy pieces behind. Deep burgundy, edge-to-edge square
background. No phone, page, type, marks, transparent corners, or baked-in outer
rounding. Platforms may apply their own icon masks. Small icon derivatives may
be generated from this master as needed.

VISUAL VERIFICATION
The master was opened and compared with the reference's 332 × 332 pixel icon
crop, side by side at equal displayed size and at 60 and 32 pixel icon sizes.
The composition, orientation, three-piece count, materials, and overall colors
remain close to the reference. It is not pixel-identical: reflections and bevel
details differ slightly, and the reconstructed group is slightly smaller within
the square. Those are reconstruction differences, not recovery of lost detail.
The icon remains readable at 60 and 32 pixels. No deployment is asserted here.

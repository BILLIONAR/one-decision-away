## Fidelity rubric

Scores assess visible likeness, not literal copying of brands or sample data. Each dimension is scored 0–4: **0 absent; 1 colours/accent only; 2 partial resemblance; 3 close recognizable composition; 4 strong recognizable composition/material/proportion match adapted to actual ODA functions**. These are independent visual judgments, not measured source-reference pixel coordinates.

| Dimension | Weight | What must be visible |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Composition and hierarchy | 30% | Correct focal point and relative ordering/importance of scene, assistant, working panel and controls. |
| Imagery and material | 25% | Meaningful scenic/nature imagery, iridescent sculptural form, glass or pearl surface character appropriate to each reference. |
| Shape and proportions | 15% | Organic curved transitions, cover-to-sheet boundaries, dominant versus supporting element sizes, coherent card silhouettes. |
| Palette and contrast character | 10% | Reference-appropriate light lavender, navy/violet or teal/cyan atmosphere with readable real text. |
| Spacing, typography and control geometry | 20% | Dense versus spacious rhythm matches the reference area; relevant titles and primary actions have the right prominence. |

Acceptance requires **at least 80/100 for each of the three groups** (Sound refs 1–3; Coach refs 4–5; working surfaces refs 6–7), **at least 3/4 in composition and imagery/material for each group**, and a visible pass for every mandatory row above. A high colour score cannot compensate for missing scenes, central assistant or date-led working composition. The final parent/user visual judgment remains authoritative; the rubric is a review aid, not approval.

Functional/accessibility gates remain separate: actual data and state, no unsupported features or copied third-party content; safe storage/draft preservation, truthful English guidance/device mode, existing native timer text-fit repair, real timer/reward deduplication, keyboard/focus/reduced motion, appropriate contrast and 44px relevant targets. Passing these gates alone is not fidelity acceptance.

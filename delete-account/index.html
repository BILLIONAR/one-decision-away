Delete your ODA account

You can delete your ODA account and its cloud records without a subscription.

In the app

Open Settings → Delete account, then choose Delete my account on the Account page. Sign in to the account you want to delete and confirm. You can also reach Account from the subscription and locked screens.

Without the app

Email Yahya Labs from the email address used for your ODA account and ask to delete your account. You do not need to explain why. We verify ownership before deleting account records.

What is deleted

Deletion removes your sign-in account and associated cloud backup, saved app records, coach memory, feedback and usage records. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy. Use Settings, Reset all data to erase records stored only on this device.

Your store subscription

Account deletion does not cancel an Apple or Google Play subscription. Cancel separately in the store where you subscribed. Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat may retain purchase records needed for billing or legal obligations. Copies in infrastructure backups may remain until their normal retention expires.
